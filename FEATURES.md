# Part A: Two core features Coy should have

Coy's promise is simple: **tell Coy who you like; if they like you back, you both find out, otherwise nobody ever knows.** Both ideas below keep that rule. Nothing new is revealed unless it is mutual.

---

## 1. Add a "why" and optional questions when adding a crush

### What

Instead of only sending a message that someone has a crush on them, the person adding a crush gets a space to say **why** that person is their crush, and to answer some **template questions** for them. The questions are optional.

### Why it is needed

Right now the person only learns that "someone has a crush on you". That is easy to ignore or doubt. Knowing why makes the crush feel real and personal, and it gives them a reason to join Coy and add the person back.

### Privacy

The reason and the answers stay hidden until **both people have added each other**. The anonymous heads-up still reveals nothing, so Coy's promise is kept.

---

## 2. Conversation tags at the start of a chat, visible only if both chose them

### What

When two people like each other they get to chat. This takes that mutual liking one step further: at the start of the chat, each person can pick tags such as **"coffee on weekends"**, **"a quick call"** or **"movie this Friday"**. A tag is only visible if **both** people chose it.

### Why it is needed

It removes the hesitation of starting the conversation. Nobody has to make the first move or risk a "no", because you only see what you both already want.

---

### How these relate to the Discover tab

The Discover tab in this repository already follows the same rule in several places: likes stay secret until mutual, there is no "who liked you" list, and a match unlocks the chat. Idea 2 is a natural next step there, since a chat opens with conversation starters built from what two people have in common.
